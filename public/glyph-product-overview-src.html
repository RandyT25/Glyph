02 / How It Works
The Solution
Tap. Stamp. Reward.
Under 2 seconds.

Glyph uses NFC chips — the same technology as contactless payments — to stamp a digital loyalty card with a single phone tap. No app download needed. No friction.

01
Merchant registers an NFC tag
A small NFC sticker or puck is registered to a campaign in the Glyph dashboard in under 30 seconds. Assign it to a location, set your stamp target, and you're live.
Setup in under 30 seconds
02
Customer taps their phone
The customer holds their phone near the NFC tag. A web page opens automatically — no app required. They tap 'Stamp' and the loyalty card updates instantly on their screen.
No app download required
03
Reward unlocks automatically
When the customer reaches their stamp target (e.g. 9 stamps), a unique QR reward code is generated. The merchant scans it at the counter — fast, secure, and fraud-proof.
QR code redemption
